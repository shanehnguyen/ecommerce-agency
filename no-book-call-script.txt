NO-BOOK RESCUE CALL SCRIPT
Goal: calendar invite accepted while still on the phone.
This is a 3-minute booking call, NOT a sales call. The free
design is the pitch. They already applied. Don't sell.
=============================================================

BEFORE YOU DIAL
=============================================================
[ ] Text already sent earlier (so your number rings familiar)
[ ] Their name: ______________
[ ] Store name if you know it: ______________
[ ] Two slots to offer: ____________ and ____________
[ ] Between 8am and 9pm THEIR time


THE CALL
=============================================================

THEY ANSWER:

YOU: "Hey [name], it's Shane - you applied for the free
homepage design earlier. Figured it was faster to call than
keep texting. Got two minutes?"

(If they sound confused / "who?"):
YOU: "Shane - the free Shopify homepage design, you filled
out the application on your phone earlier today. Ringing a
bell?"

THEY SAY SURE:

YOU: "So what's the store - what are you selling?"

*** SHUT UP AND LISTEN FOR 60 SECONDS. ***
React to ONE specific thing they say:
"Oh nice - [specific]. That's a good lane."

THEN BOOK - say this right after they finish:

YOU: "Cool. Next step's easy - a 30 minute call where I dig
into everything and you walk away with the free homepage
design. I've got [SLOT 1] or [SLOT 2] - which works?"

THEY PICK ONE:

YOU: "Perfect - sending the invite right now, hang on...
[SEND IT WHILE ON THE PHONE]
...should be in your email. See it?"

Wait for the yes. THEN:

YOU: "Locked. Between now and then shoot me the store link
so I can dig in before we talk. Talk [day]."

Hang up. Done. Do not keep chatting.


BRANCHES
=============================================================

"I'M SLAMMED RIGHT NOW":
YOU: "Totally - twenty seconds then: I design your homepage
free, you look at it, if you love it we talk about the full
store, if not we're square. When's better - tonight or
tomorrow?"
(Still a WHICH time question, never a WHETHER question.)

"ACTUALLY NOW WORKS":
Grab the intro call sheet and run it right there. A live
lead beats a scheduled one. If you truly can't, book the
nearest slot: "Let's lock tomorrow so I can dig into your
store first - that's where the good stuff comes from."

"HOW MUCH DOES IT COST?":
YOU: "The homepage design is actually free - no card,
nothing. If you love it and want the full store built,
that's when we'd talk numbers, and I'll give you the exact
price on the call. If you don't love it, you owe me nothing.
So - [SLOT 1] or [SLOT 2]?"
(Answer, reassure, re-ask. Don't quote the build price on
this call - price goes with the walkthrough.)

"I'LL BOOK ON THE CALENDAR LATER":
YOU: "The link works, but honestly it's faster if I just do
it now while I've got you - [SLOT 1] or [SLOT 2]?"
(If they still resist, don't push twice:) "All good - I'll
text you the link right after this. Grab whatever works."
Then day-4 takeaway text if they never book.

"NOT INTERESTED ANYMORE":
YOU: "No stress. Out of curiosity, what changed?"
(Whatever they say is free market research - write it down.)
"Fair enough. If the store site ever moves back up the list,
you know where I am. Good luck with it."
Mark dead. Do not add to any more sequences.

NO ANSWER:
Do not leave a long voicemail. Missed call + your earlier
text IS the message. If you leave anything, 5 seconds:
"It's Shane about the homepage design - text me back when
you're free."
One more call attempt day 2 or 3 at a DIFFERENT time of day.
Then day-4 takeaway text. Then done.


AFTER THE CALL - LOG IT

Booked for: ____________
Store link received: Y / N
Notes / their words: ______________________________________


RULES
=============================================================
1. Two minutes of you talking, max. Every extra minute of
   explaining gives them room to find a maybe.
2. Never ask IF they want to book. Ask WHICH time.
3. Invite sent and confirmed before you hang up, or it
   didn't happen.
4. Two call attempts total across the sequence. More = spam.
5. "Stop calling" = final. Mark it, move on.
