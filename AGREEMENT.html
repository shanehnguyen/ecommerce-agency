2. Project & Scope

FlipFix will design and build a complete Shopify storefront for the Client ("the Build"), based on the homepage design approved by the Client on [DESIGN APPROVAL DATE]. The Build includes:

  • Homepage (per the approved design)
  • Collection page template
  • Product page template, designed mobile-first
  • Cart drawer
  • Four supporting pages
  • Conversion strategy applied throughout (research, copy direction, layout)
  • Reviews configured and imported
  • Analytics installed and verified
  • Accelerated checkout (e.g., Shop Pay / Apple Pay) enabled and positioned
  • Performance pass — target mobile PageSpeed 60+, LCP under 2.5 seconds
  • Full quality-assurance review, including a live test purchase
  • Handover walkthrough and documentation

Not included (available as separately quoted add-ons): platform migration, copywriting beyond the Build scope, photography or videography, additional page templates, custom app development, ongoing testing or maintenance.

5. Guarantees

5.1  Delivery guarantee. If the store is not live within the 14-day window (Day 0 through Launch, as adjusted under Section 6) due to FlipFix's delay, the Client's remaining balance of $2,250 is waived.

5.2  Performance guarantee. The parties recorded the store's baseline together on Day 0: [X.X]% conversion rate over the trailing 30 days, per [ANALYTICS SOURCE]. After launch, FlipFix will continue iterating on the store at no additional charge until its conversion rate exceeds this baseline, measured over a comparable period under the conditions in Section 6.3.

5.3  Code warranty. Any defect in code written by FlipFix will be corrected at no charge for six (6) months following launch.

6. Schedule & Measurement Conditions

6.1  Clock pauses. Days spent waiting on the Client's assets, feedback, approvals, or access pause the 14-day clock; it resumes when the needed item is received.

6.2  Feedback round. Client feedback on the build is provided in one consolidated round on the date in Section 3. Later change requests are welcome and will be handled during the post-launch iteration period, so the launch date holds.

6.3  Fair comparison. The performance guarantee compares like with like: same store, same analytics tool, and materially similar traffic sources and promotional activity as the baseline period. If the Client makes major changes (e.g., pausing all advertising, running a site-wide sale absent from the baseline period, or migrating platforms), the parties will record a new baseline together and the guarantee continues against it.
