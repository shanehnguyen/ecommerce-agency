MINUTES 0-2: OPEN AND SET THE FRAME
=============================================================

Small talk for 30 seconds max, then take the wheel:

SAY: "Here's how I want to use the 30 minutes. First I'll ask
you about the brand and where you're trying to take it. Then
I've got some quick questions I need answered to actually
design this thing for you. Then I'll tell you what I'd build,
walk you through how the free design works, what it costs if
you want the full store after, and we'll set next steps.

MINUTES 20-24: WHAT I'D BUILD
=============================================================

homepage needs to do..."

exact words from the first question.

MINUTES 24-27: PROCESS AND PRICE
=============================================================

SAY: "Here's how this works. I design your homepage first.

of you by [DATE FROM PREP].

We get on a call, I walk you through it. If you love it, the
full store build is [PRICE] and your store is live in 14 days.
If you don't love it, you owe me nothing and you keep the
ideas."

MINUTES 27-30: NEXT STEPS AND CLOSE
=============================================================

Spell out next steps so there is zero ambiguity. Then book the
review call ON THIS CALL. Never "I'll send times over email."

SAY: "So here's exactly what happens next.

One - tonight I'm texting you a recap plus a short list of
what I need from you: logo if you have one, product photos,
and that story you just told me.

Two - you send me that stuff by [DAY, ~2 days out].

Three - I design the homepage. You see it [DATE].

Four - let's lock the walkthrough call right now. Does [DAY]
at this same time work?"
