# Free Design Onboarding — Day 0 to Day 5

What happens between Call 1 (discovery) and Call 2 (the reveal/close). The design is the sales asset — this system is how it gets made in 5 days without eating your week.

Budget per free design: **6–8 hours total.** More than that and the unit economics break (a free design should cost you hours, not days).

---

## The pipeline at a glance

| Day | What | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Day 0 | Call 1 → intake email within the hour | 15 min |
| Day 0–2 | They send assets (48h deadline — clock pauses if late) | — |
| Day 1–2 | Research block | 1.5–2 hrs |
| Day 2–4 | Design the homepage concept | 4–5 hrs |
| Day 4 | Build the reveal: side-by-side + notes | 1 hr |
| Day 5 | CALL 2 — the reveal | 30 min |
| Day 5–12 | Decision window (nudge day 2, final day 5–6, release day 7) | — |

Max free designs in flight at once: **2.** A third fit caller books their Call 1 for next week — that's the real scarcity working.

---

## Step 1 — Intake email (within 1 hour of Call 1)

Send this, personalized only in the bracketed spots:

> **Subject: Your free homepage design — 2 things to start**
>
> [Name] — good call today. Your design clock starts as soon as I have these two things:
>
> 1. **Logo + brand files** — whatever you have (logo files, fonts, colors if they exist)
> 2. **Product photos** — a folder/link with your best ones, especially [their hero product]
>
> That's it. Get me these within 48 hours and you'll see your new homepage on our call [reveal date].
>
> Already booked: **[reveal date/time]**. That's where you see it next to your current site and decide. Until then you owe nothing.
>
> — Shane

**Rules:**
- 48-hour asset deadline, stated in the email. If assets are late: one nudge at 24h. If still nothing by 72h, message: "No rush — but the 5 days starts when the assets land, so I've moved our reveal to [new date]." The clock pause protects your calendar; saying it protects the relationship.
- Everything into one client folder the day it arrives: `clients/[brand]/` — assets, the Call-1 baseline screenshots, your notes. One place, always the same structure.

---

## Step 2 — Research block (1.5–2 hrs, timeboxed)

This is the "we research your customers first" promise. Timebox it — research expands to fill whatever you give it.

- [ ] **Reviews (30 min):** their product reviews + 1–2 competitors'. You're hunting exact customer phrases — what they praise, what they doubted before buying, what surprised them. Copy 5–10 quotes into the notes file. These become the design's headlines and answered objections.
- [ ] **Their ads (15 min):** Meta Ad Library. What promise does the ad make? The homepage's first screen must match it — this is message match, and it's the #1 thing their current homepage gets wrong.
- [ ] **Their numbers (from Call 1):** you captured the baseline on the screen-share — while there, you also glanced at device split and top products. Design for the device and product that actually gets the traffic. (No analytics access needed for the design; store access comes at kickoff, after payment.)
- [ ] **Competitors (30 min):** the 2–3 stores they mentioned on Call 1 (or category leaders). What proof/trust elements do the winners use that this store lacks?
- [ ] Write the 5-line brief at the top of the notes file: **who buys, why they hesitate, what the ad promises, hero product, the one number to beat.** The design follows the brief, not vibes.

---

## Step 3 — The design (4–5 hrs, from template, never blank canvas)

Build ONE homepage concept from your standard skeleton, customized by the brief:

1. **Hero** — matches their ad's promise, their product, one CTA
2. **Proof strip** — reviews/ratings/logos immediately after the hero
3. **Best sellers / hero product block** — their real photos
4. **The objection section** — answers the top doubt from the review mining, in customer language
5. **Trust block** — shipping, returns, guarantees answered early
6. **Final CTA**

Rules:
- **Mobile-first, always** — design the phone version first; that's where their traffic is (you checked in Step 2).
- **Their real content** — real products, real quotes from real reviews, real prices. A design with lorem ipsum doesn't close; a design that looks like their store already upgraded does.
- **One concept, not three.** You're the expert; options transfer the work back to them. The revision round after approval handles preferences.
- Stop at good-enough-to-obviously-beat-their-current-page. Day-14 polish happens after they pay.

---

## Step 4 — Build the reveal (1 hr, day before Call 2)

- [ ] **Side-by-side:** their current homepage and yours, same screen, mobile frames. This one image is the close.
- [ ] **Annotate 3–4 callouts,** each tied to something they said on Call 1: "you said people ask about X — answered here," "your ad promises Y — first screen now matches it."
- [ ] Have their money number (from Call 1's "what's half a percent worth?") written at the top of your notes. You open the reveal with it.
- [ ] Invoice for $2,250 drafted and ready to send during the call.

Do NOT send the design before the call. The reveal IS the close — emailed designs get judged alone, without the story, and ghosted. If they ask to see it early: "It's ready — I want to walk you through the why, it's 20 minutes, that's the whole point of doing this free."

---

## Step 5 — The decision window (after Call 2)

Approved on the call → invoice sent on the call, kickoff booked on the call. Done.

Not yet:
- **Day 2:** one nudge — "Any questions on the design? Happy to hop on for 10 minutes."
- **Day 5–6:** the final — "Slot releases [date]. If it's a yes, the invoice link is here and your store's live 9 days after payment. If it's a no, no hard feelings — just tell me so I can give the slot away."
- **Day 7:** release. Move the next store up. Never chase past the window — the window being real is what makes it work next time.

Declined → ask exactly one question: "What was the miss?" (Their answer improves the next design.) Keep the concept — it's portfolio material and future ad creative.

---

## After approval → Call 3 (kickoff) → build days 5–14

Onboarding ends at paid. From kickoff: final asset check, baseline re-confirmed, real dates for the feedback round / QA + live test purchase / launch. One consolidated feedback round. Client delays pause the clock — said at kickoff, in writing after.
